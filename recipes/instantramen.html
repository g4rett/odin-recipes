Instant Ramen Made Better

Home Pizza Recipe

A recipe for instant ramen? Seriously?

Yes, seriously. While making some instant ramen is as simple
as pouring hot water over your noodles and adding the seasoning
packet, this recipe aims to transform this boring dish into a tasty meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 instant ramen pack
  • Sriracha Sauce (2 tablespoons)
  • Garlic Powder (2 teaspoons)
  • Soy Sauce (1 teaspoon)
  • Sesame Oil (1 teaspoon)
  • Any meat of your choosing (sliced or chopped)
  • Optional Hot sauce of your choosing (for extra heat)

Directions

  1. Boil the noodles

    Add your noodles to boiling water and boil for 3 to 5 minutes. You may discard the seasoning packet that came with it as it's not necessary for this dish.

  2. Strain noodles from the water

    Use a strainer or carefully pour out the water from the pot and transfer the cooked noodles to a bowl.

  3. Add your ingredients

    Begin adding your ingredients in the amounts listed above. Skip adding the meat for now.
